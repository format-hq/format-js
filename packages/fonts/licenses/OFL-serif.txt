ABOUT FMT CRIMSON PRO

FMT Crimson Pro is a Modified Version of Crimson Pro. The outlines
and variation data of Crimson Pro are reused unchanged; the
modification is limited to a +10% apparent glyph-size increase,
achieved by scaling all geometry and variation deltas (including
HVAR and MVAR) and then resetting the units-per-em back to the
original value of 1024. This brings the cap-height from 57.3% of
the em up to 63.0% of the em, in line with conventional text-face
proportions, for improved on-screen and e-reader legibility at
typical body sizes.

The weight axis (wght 200-900) and all eight named instances of the
original variable font are preserved in both roman and italic. The
italic angle of the italic variant (-12 degrees) is preserved
unchanged.

The modifications were applied directly to the Crimson Pro variable
source using fontTools, preserving all variable-font data structures.
