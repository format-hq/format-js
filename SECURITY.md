# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately, not through a public issue: use [GitHub's private advisory form](https://github.com/format-hq/format-js/security/advisories/new), or email **security@format.dev**.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and which package and version you were on. A proof of concept helps but is not required to report.

You will get an acknowledgement within three working days. We will tell you what we found, what we intend to do, and when — and we will credit you in the advisory unless you would rather we did not.

## What is in scope

The packages published from this repository, at their current versions. The rendering engine and the hosted API are covered too: report them here and they reach the same people.

Out of scope: findings that need an already-compromised machine, and reports produced by a scanner without a demonstrated impact.

## Supported versions

Format is pre-1.0, and fixes land on the current release rather than being backported. If you are on an older version, upgrading is the fix.
